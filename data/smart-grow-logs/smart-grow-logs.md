## Overview

Smart Grow Logs is a centralized log management platform with end-to-end encryption, real-time alerts, and multi-platform SDKs.

## Pricing

The free tier includes up to 3,000 logs per day.

## Features

- Centralized log management
- End-to-end encryption
- Real-time alerts
- Multi-platform SDKs
- Up to 3,000 logs per day on the free tier
