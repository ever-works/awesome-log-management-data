## Overview

Logstash is a free and open server-side data processing pipeline that ingests data from a multitude of sources simultaneously, transforms it, and then sends it to your favorite stash.

## Features

- Ingest data from a multitude of sources simultaneously
- Transform data with a wide range of filters
- Send data to a wide variety of destinations
- Plugin architecture for inputs, filters, and codecs, and outputs
- Apache 2.0 licensed
