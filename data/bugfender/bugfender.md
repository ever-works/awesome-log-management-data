## Overview

Bugfender is a remote logging and crash reporting service for mobile and web applications.

## Pricing

The free plan includes up to 100k log lines per day with 24 hours of retention.

## Features

- Remote logging for mobile and web applications
- Up to 100k log lines/day on the free plan
- 24 hours log retention on the free plan
- Integrations with platforms like Vercel
- Advanced data querying
- Email and Discord notifiers
