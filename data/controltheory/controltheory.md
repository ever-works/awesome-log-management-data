# ControlTheory

## Overview
ControlTheory is an observability platform that natively supports OpenTelemetry via OTLP. It offers observability pipelines and data management capabilities with a mixed open-source and commercial model.

## Features
- Native OpenTelemetry support via OTLP
- Observability pipelines
- Data management and routing
- Mixed open-source and commercial offering

## Pricing
Mixed open-source and commercial.

## Deployment
Self-hosted or cloud.
