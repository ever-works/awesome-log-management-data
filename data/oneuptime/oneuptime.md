# OneUptime

## Overview
OneUptime is an observability platform that natively supports OpenTelemetry via OTLP. It combines monitoring, logging, tracing, and incident management in a single platform for application reliability.

## Features
- Native OpenTelemetry support via OTLP
- Application monitoring
- Log management
- Distributed tracing
- Incident management
- Status pages
- Open-source and commercial offerings

## Pricing
Mixed open-source and commercial; free tier available.

## Deployment
Self-hosted or cloud.
