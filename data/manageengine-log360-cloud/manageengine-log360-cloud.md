## Overview

ManageEngine Log360 Cloud is a cloud-based log management service powered by ManageEngine.

## Pricing

The free plan offers 50 GB storage with 15 days of storage retention and 7 days of search.

## Features

- Cloud-based log management
- 50 GB storage on the free plan
- 15 days of storage retention on the free plan
- 7 days of search on the free plan
